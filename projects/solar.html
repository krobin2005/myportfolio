BMA Solar Feasibility Study

Could Burke Mountain Academy power its campus with the sun? I co-led a study to find out what it would take and what it would cost.

RoleCo-Lead Researcher & Project Analyst
WhenJan – May 2024
ToolsExcel, financial modeling, policy research
$1.6M Proposed project
248K+ kWh of annual campus use targeted
3 Federal incentives evaluated

Overview

Burke Mountain Academy uses more than 248,000 kWh of electricity a year. Our team evaluated whether a $1.6M solar installation could offset that demand, looking at system sizing, project economics, and whether it could realistically be implemented on campus.

What I did

  • Co-led the research and analysis for the proposal from start to finish.
  • Evaluated system sizing and project economics to test the project's feasibility.
  • Researched federal clean-energy incentives, including IRA Elective Pay, the Investment Tax Credit (ITC), and the Production Tax Credit (PTC), along with Vermont grant opportunities to reduce project costs.
  • Developed strategies to bring real-time solar production data into environmental science classes and campus energy audits.

Why it mattered

Beyond lowering energy costs, the proposal treated the array as a teaching tool, so students could learn from live data generated on their own campus.
