Industrial Equity Pitches

Researching industrial companies and turning the numbers into investment recommendations for the Bates Investment Club.

RoleIndustrial Sector Analyst
WhenAug 2025 – Present
ToolsExcel, DCF, comps

Overview

As an analyst on the club's industrial sector team, I research companies, build valuation models, and help turn that analysis into stock pitches that the club votes on.

How a pitch comes together

  • Fundamental research: understanding the business, its industry, and its financial statements.
  • Discounted cash flow (DCF): projecting future cash flows and discounting them back to estimate what the company is worth today.
  • Comparable-company analysis: checking that value against how the market prices similar companies.
  • The pitch: working with teammates to build an investment thesis and present an actionable recommendation.

What it connects to

Valuation is a modeling problem: you make assumptions, test how sensitive the answer is to them, and decide how much to trust the result. It's the same kind of thinking I use in data science and optimization.
