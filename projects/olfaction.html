Digital Olfaction Agent

Using AI agents to help map and measure the sense of smell, connecting how people perceive odors with how mouse neural networks encode them.

RoleLead of AI Research
WhenSep 2026 – Present
ToolsPython, Claude API, Pyrfume

The question

Vision and hearing have well-established ways to measure what we sense, like wavelength and frequency. Smell doesn't have an equivalent. Professor Jason Castro's lab studies how to map and objectify the sense of smell by bridging two kinds of data: human psychophysics (how people describe and rate odors) and mouse neural networks (how the olfactory system encodes them).

What I'm doing

  • Leading development of AI agent interfaces that help the lab explore large olfactory datasets.
  • Engineering Python-based LLM workflows with the Claude API and custom hook functions.
  • Optimizing token usage so agents can securely and efficiently query large external databases like Pyrfume, an open collection of olfactory datasets.
  • Collaborating across the lab to analyze machine learning outputs.

Where it's headed

We're working toward a co-authored publication in digital olfaction by the end of the semester.
