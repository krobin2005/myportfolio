Learning log

My explorations from DCS340's Introduction to Classical Methods in Optimization module: finding peaks and valleys with code, one method at a time.

01
1D Optimization · Sep 16, 2026

The Pumpkin Problem: Bisection vs. Newton's Method

Modeled a launched pumpkin's path as f(x) = 4 − (x − 2)² and found its peak two ways. Bisection repeatedly halves an interval until the derivative's sign change is pinned down; Newton's method uses the first and second derivatives to jump straight to it.

"It reminded me heavily of merge sort. Optimization accuracy depends not only on the formula, but also on the method's assumptions and the quality of the initial guess."

2D Optimization

Finding the Bottom of the Bowl

Moved up a dimension to the surface f(x, y) = (x − 2)² − xy + (y − 3)². Visualized it as a 3D surface and contour "topo map," then compared three routes to the minimum: SciPy's Nelder–Mead, Newton's method with a hand-derived gradient and Hessian, and my own gradient descent loop.
